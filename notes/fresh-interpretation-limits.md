# Limits fixed during integrity checking, without changing fresh methods

An independent source-text audit of seed 601 confirmed the narrow revision
changes one complete request's target and the broad revision changes five.
The combined branch contains six foreign-fee queries and two old-family queries,
but none of its eight targets differ from v0. The corpus is revised in that
branch, yet its sampled answers are unaffected by the edits. Retain this sample
and report the denominator; do not selectively regenerate it to strengthen an
interaction claim. This branch cannot establish protection against consequential
content changes and evidence changes in the same answer. Narrow/broad branches
provide the actual consequential-revision tests.

The two acquisition lengths also have different seeds. Any 16-versus-32
comparison describes those sequences and does not isolate reuse length from
sampling variation. The central comparison remains paired across retrieval arms
within each fresh request. Named-account requests make exact entity lookup
available to all methods, intentionally exposing a competitive cheap control.
These findings will not generalize automatically to implicit entities, long
conversations, cross-account recommendations or larger collections.

Seed 602's frozen metadata likewise has no affected target in the combined
branch. Its narrow/broad branches have two affected requests each. Thus the
fresh sample contains ten genuinely changed request targets in total (six in
601, four in 602), and neither combined branch supports a consequential joint-
change claim. This is a sampling limitation, preserved without replacement.
